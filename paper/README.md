# [Counting Sources, Not Bursts](paper.pdf)

**Author:** David Lindgreen · **Date:** October 9, 2026 · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Status:** independent re-analysis of one public catalog. The v0.1 registry is preregistered and reproduces exactly
from a fresh download of the catalog; the source-clustering diagnostics (`reports/v0.2-source-clustering.json`) are
post-hoc and labelled as such. **Not peer reviewed.** The paper also records a correction to this repository's earlier
documentation (a wrong per-source burst count; see the erratum in `docs/research-report.md`). The analysis code, tests and
drafts were produced with substantial AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic); the author is responsible for the content.
